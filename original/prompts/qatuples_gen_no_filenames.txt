    **QUESTION:**
    Which functionalities of the NWDAF are listed in clause 4.1 after the proposed change?
    
    **ANSWER:**
    The NWDAF functionalities include:

    * Data collection from AMF, SMF, UPF, PCF, UDM, NSACF, AF (directly or via NEF), NRF, SCP, and OAM.
    * Analytics and data collection using DCCF.
    * Retrieval from data repositories (UDR via UDM or optionally via NEF(PFDF)).
    * Data collection of location information via LCS and LMF.
    * Storage and retrieval from ADRF.
    * Analytics and data collection from MFAF.
    * Retrieval of information about NFs (from NRF).
    * On-demand provision of analytics.
    * Provision of bulked data related to Analytics IDs.
    * Provision of accuracy information about Analytics IDs.
    * Provision or retrieval of ML model accuracy or degradation information.
    * Vertical Federated Learning.

    **QUESTION:**
    What potential issues or consequences could arise if this change request is not approved?
    
    **ANSWER:**
    If the proposed change is not approved, there will be a documented inconsistency between clauses 4.1 and 6.22 of the related specifications. This misalignment may lead to confusion or incorrect implementation by vendors or network operators. NWDAF implementations might miss out on critical data sources like NRF and SCP for signaling storm analytics, potentially leading to incomplete or inaccurate analytics functionality, and reduced capability in detecting or managing signaling storms.
    
    **QUESTION:**
    What is the reason for the proposed change in CR 1380 rev for TS 28 8, and what misalignment does it address?
    
    **ANSWER:**
    The reason for the proposed change is that the Network Data Analytics Function (NWDAF), as described in clause 6.22 of TS 23.288, is defined to collect data from the Network Repository Function (NRF) and the Service Communication Proxy (SCP) for signaling storm analytics. However, this data collection capability was not reflected in clause 4.1 of the current specification. This omission creates a misalignment between clauses 4.1 and 6.22, as well as an inconsistency in the documented data collection sources. The change request aims to correct this by formally adding NRF and SCP as data collection sources in clause 4.1.
    
    **QUESTION:**
    What is the detailed summary of the proposed changes introduced by CR 1380 rev?
    
    **ANSWER:**
    The change request proposes to update clause 4.1 of the specification to reflect the full range of NWDAF data collection sources. Specifically, it adds NRF and SCP to the list of potential data sources for NWDAF. The updated clause 4.1 will clearly state that NWDAF can collect data based on event subscriptions not only from AMF, SMF, UPF, PCF, UDM, NSACF, AF (directly or via NEF), and OAM services, but also from NRF and SCP. This change aligns the clause with what is defined elsewhere in TS 23.288, ensuring consistency and completeness.
